Comparing

What this tool does. It takes what the client in front of you needs, checks it against the controlled Good Better Best range, and says plainly which products meet every requirement, which fall short and on what, and where the range has nothing that fits.

What it does not do. It does not replace a clinical assessment, a prescription or an occupational therapy recommendation. Safe working load, seat width and pressure care selection must be confirmed against the current product documentation before an order is placed.

Availability. Display and stock status is recorded per showroom and is only as current as the last update shown on the Catalogue tab.
